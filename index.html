Our mission

Social impact is the core value all of our work revolves around. Our members come from every corner of WashU, with different ambitions and skill sets. What we share is a goal to make a more compassionate, sustainable world, one step at a time.

Join a project team

Members work in teams of a project manager and four to five consultants, on client meetings, market research, data analysis, and client-facing presentations.

  • No consulting experience required
  • 6–8 hours a week after onboarding
