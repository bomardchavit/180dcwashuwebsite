180 Degrees Consulting WashU

Developing solutions in Saint Louis, for Saint Louis.

180 Degrees Consulting is the world's largest student consulting organization for non-profits and social enterprises. With 170+ branches worldwide, we provide sustainable, innovative services to socially-conscious organizations.

Our Mission

Social impact is the core value which all our branch’s work revolves around as we work with non-profits and social enterprises. Our members come from a wide array of disciplines, have unique ambitions, and have many different skill sets. Despite these differences, what each member of 180 DC WashU shares is a goal to make a more compassionate, sustainable world, one step at a time.
