Since 2024, we've had big ambitions.

Founded in the summer of 2024, we saw a part of Saint Louis too often overlooked. Small businesses and nonprofits are the backbone of Saint Louis communities, yet they often lack the resources to get outside help. Enter 180 DC WashU: our pro-bono services give clients the assistance they deserve, and give our members a way to make a meaningful impact in their community.

We're the first social impact-driven consulting organization on WashU's campus. As a young branch, we plan to grow our projects in both number and scope, and with 180 Degrees Consulting's global resources behind us, we're working toward a more prosperous Saint Louis.
